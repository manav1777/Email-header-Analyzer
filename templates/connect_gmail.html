Need help connecting Gmail?

MailGuard requires a Google App Password. Your regular Gmail password will not work.

Use a Google App Password instead of your normal Gmail password.
✓ MailGuard uses your Gmail credentials only to establish the email connection through IMAP. Never enter your regular Google account password into MailGuard.

How to Connect Gmail

1

Make sure 2 Step Verification is enabled

Open your Google Account security settings and make sure 2 Step Verification is enabled for your account.

2

Create a Google App Password

In your Google Account, open App Passwords and create a new password for MailGuard. Google will generate a 16 character password.

3

Enter your Gmail address

Enter the Gmail address of the inbox you want MailGuard to analyze.

4

Enter the App Password

Copy the generated Google App Password and paste it into the App Password field in MailGuard.

Important security note

Do not enter your normal Gmail password. MailGuard requires a Google App Password because Google does not allow standard Gmail passwords for this type of IMAP connection.
